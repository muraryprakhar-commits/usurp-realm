Questions

Is this my family's real coat of arms?

No, and be wary of sites that say otherwise. In English and Scottish heraldry, arms belong to one person and their descendants, not to everyone who shares a surname. Crest Studio helps you design an original coat of arms for your family as it is today, using real heraldic colors, rules and symbols, and tells you what every part means.

What do I get for $9?

Print-ready art (a 4000 x 5000 px PNG, a web-size PNG and an SVG vector that scales to any size), a printable certificate of arms with your blazon and its meaning, a 1080 px profile picture and a phone wallpaper. The files download the moment payment goes through.

Do I need an account?

No. Designing is free in your browser with no sign-up, and your design is saved on your device. You only give an email and billing details at checkout, for the receipt.

Can I print it on mugs, shirts or canvas?

Yes. The files are yours to use for any lawful purpose, with any print shop or print-on-demand service. The 4000 px file prints sharply at about 13 by 16 inches, and the SVG scales to any size.
