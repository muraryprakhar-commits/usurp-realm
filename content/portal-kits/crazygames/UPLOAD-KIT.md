# CrazyGames upload kit

Everything is in this folder: one zip per game (for example `archery.zip`), three covers per game named after it, and the listing text below.

## Before the first upload (about 10 minutes, once)
1. Open developer.crazygames.com and sign up as a developer with your own email.
2. Fill in your payout details (CrazyGames asks for these before a submission).
3. For each game: Submit a game, upload the zip, then add the three covers and the text below.

CrazyGames reviews each game. A new game starts in Basic Launch, where ads are off; games selected for Full Launch earn a share of the ads.
These builds already include the CrazyGames SDK v3: ads only at natural breaks (after a level is won or lost, never during play or on a button), sound muted during ads, gameplay start and stop events, and no links or other ads.
They are on usurp.games (your own site) and nowhere else. Check the current CrazyGames terms before also listing them on another portal.

- Description: Free trebuchet game with real physics. Aim, fling stones and fire pots, and bring down ten castles of timber and stone. Plays on phone and computer.

## Viking Chess (Hnefatafl)
- Zip: `tafl.zip`
- Covers: `tafl-landscape-1920x1080.jpg`, `tafl-portrait-800x1200.jpg`, `tafl-square-800x800.jpg`
- Category: Board
- Tags: viking chess, hnefatafl, tafl, board game, strategy, two player
- Orientation: Portrait (it also plays in landscape, centred, on desktop)
- Devices: desktop and mobile
- Short description: Lead the king to a corner, or trap him.
- Description: Play Viking chess free in your browser: defend the king or lead the raiders in Brandubh or Tablut, against the computer at three levels or a friend.
- How to play: The defenders and their king start in the middle; the raiders start around the edges. The raiders move first. Every piece moves any number of empty squares in a straight line, like a rook in chess. Only the king may stop on the throne in the middle or on a corner. Take an enemy piece by trapping it between two of yours, in a line. A corner, or the empty throne, counts as one of yours. The defenders win when the king reaches a corner. The raiders win by surrounding the king: four sides on the throne, three beside it, two anywhere else.
- Controls: Tap a piece, then a marked square. Drag also works. Keyboard: arrow keys and Space.

## Nine Men's Morris
- Zip: `morris.zip`
- Covers: `morris-landscape-1920x1080.jpg`, `morris-portrait-800x1200.jpg`, `morris-square-800x800.jpg`
- Category: Board
- Tags: nine men's morris, mills, board game, strategy, two player, classic
- Orientation: Portrait (it also plays in landscape, centred, on desktop)
- Devices: desktop and mobile
- Short description: Place, slide, make mills. Beat the Lord.
- Description: Play Nine Men's Morris free in your browser against the computer at three levels or a friend on one device. The medieval game of mills, with clear rules.
- How to play: Each player has nine pieces. Take turns placing them on the empty points where the lines meet. Three of your pieces in a row along a line is a mill. Every mill you make lets you take one of your opponent's pieces. When all eighteen are placed, take turns sliding one piece along a line to the next empty point. A player down to three pieces may fly to any empty point. Leave your opponent with two pieces, or with no move, and you win.
- Controls: Tap a point to place; tap a piece and then a glowing point to move. Keyboard: arrow keys and Space.
