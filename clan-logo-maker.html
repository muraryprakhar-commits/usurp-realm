What makes a good clan emblem

  • High contrast. Gold or silver on a dark color stays readable at 32 pixels in a member list.
  • One bold emblem. A crown, sword, star or crescent reads instantly. Your initial works too.
  • A short name. One or two words fit the banner at a large size.
  • A battle cry for a motto. Two to four words. It shows on the ribbon of the full crest and makes a good server tagline.
