Privacy Policy

Usurp is run by Murary Studios, Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. This page explains what we collect and why.

What stays in your browser

Your prototype progress (name, simulated spend, badges, Decrees) is saved only in your own browser's local storage. We do not receive it. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how people use the site, such as clicks, scrolling and page performance. Clarity sets cookies and collects device and usage data, and masks text you type. See Microsoft's privacy statement at privacy.microsoft.com. We also use Google Search Console, which tells us how the site appears in Google Search and does not track you on our site.

Purchases

If you buy the Founding Member Pass, Dodo Payments processes the payment as merchant of record. They collect your name, email, billing details and card information. We receive your name, email, country and purchase record so we can deliver the Pass and handle refunds. We never see or store your full card number.

Email

If you email us, we keep the conversation to answer you. We do not sell your data or send marketing you did not ask for.

How long we keep it

Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. Other data is deleted when no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing hello@usurp.games. You can block Clarity cookies in your browser settings.
