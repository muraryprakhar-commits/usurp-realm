What counts. A play is someone starting a game on usurp.games (in Siege, starting a castle). Finished means they reached the results screen (Nine Men's Morris, Viking chess and Siege count a finished match or castle). Weeks run Monday to Sunday in UTC, so they start at 5:30 am India time on Monday. Games embedded on other sites and the CrazyGames versions are not counted here: embeds collect nothing, and CrazyGames shows its own numbers in its developer portal. The same plays also appear in Microsoft Clarity as Smart events named play_archery, play_joust and so on.
