How to Host a Game Night With The Usurper

One person hosts, everyone else taps a link. Here is how to get a group from “what is this?” to their first council vote in a few minutes.

What you need

  • A phone, tablet or computer for each player, with an internet connection. Players do not need to be on the same Wi-Fi, or even in the same country.
  • One host. The match runs in the host's browser, so the host should be on a device that will stay awake and keep the page open until the match ends.
  • From 4 to 10 knights in a match. Computer knights fill any seats your friends do not, so you can play with as few as two people.

Setting up, step by step

  1. The host opens The Usurper and chooses Private room.
  2. The keep's lobby shows a four-letter room code. Tap Copy invite link and paste it into your group chat, or use the WhatsApp button.
  3. Friends open the link. It takes them straight into the room. If someone types the code instead, that works too.
  4. Everyone can change their name in the lobby, so the council knows who is who.
  5. The host sets how many knights are in the match with the minus and plus buttons. Seats not taken by friends go to computer knights.
  6. When everyone is in, the host taps Start the match.

Explaining the game in one minute

Read this out before the first match:

  • “Most of us are loyal knights. Walk the keep and do your tasks. Heavy tasks need several knights heaving together.”
  • “One of us is secretly the Usurper” (two, with seven or more knights). “The Usurper strikes knights down when nobody is watching and sabotages the keep.”
  • “If you find a fallen knight, raise the alarm. The council meets, we talk and we vote someone out.”
  • “Knights win by securing the keep or banishing every Usurper. The Usurper wins by matching our numbers, or if the postern gate stays open too long.”

The rest people learn by playing. Struck knights come back as ghosts who can still finish tasks, so nobody sits out for long.

Tips for a good night

  • Play a warm-up. Anyone who wants to practice can start a match against computer knights with Play now before the group arrives.
  • Talk in the council, not before. Private rooms allow free typing during the council. If you are in the same room, agree to save accusations for the council, so the vote matters.
  • Use the strength numbers. Every knight has a strength of 2 to 4. Say yours in the council: the Usurper has to invent one, and a lie shows up at the next heave.
  • Keep the host's page open. If the host closes the page or loses their connection, the match ends for everyone.
  • It suits mixed ages. There is no blood: struck knights simply fall. Public matches have no free typing; in a private room the chat is just your group.

If someone cannot connect

Some school and office networks block the direct connections the game uses. If a player cannot join, try mobile data instead of that Wi-Fi. If online play is unavailable on the host's network, the game says so and offers a match against computer knights instead.
