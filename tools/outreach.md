# Outreach log (gift guides, resource lists, editors who invite pitches)

Rules for the weekly autopilot:
- Only contact people whose own public page asks for pitches, submissions or suggestions by email (gift guide submission pages,
  "write for us" or "submit a resource" pages, newsletters asking for tips). Never friends, classmates or personal contacts,
  never addresses taken from directories, scraped lists or blurred "reveal email" sites.
- One email per target. At most one polite follow-up after 10 or more days with no reply. Stop at once if they say no.
- Every email: personalized to their page, a sample crest made for them (tools/press.py), honest facts (original designs,
  not historical arms), and a line saying they can ask not to hear from us again.
- Create Gmail drafts only. Never send. Prakhar sends them.
- Before drafting, search Gmail (in:sent OR in:draft) for the address to avoid duplicates, and log it below.
