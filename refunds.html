Refund Policy

14-day refund

If you change your mind about the Founding Member Pass, email hello@usurp.games within 14 days of purchase with the email you used at checkout. We refund the full amount to your original payment method. No reason needed.

What happens after a refund

Your Founding Member badge and crest are removed, your name comes off the Founders roll, and the refunded amount no longer counts toward your ladder standing.

After 14 days

Refunds after 14 days are not offered, except where the law requires it or where we close Usurp within 12 months of your purchase.

Timing

Refunds are issued through Dodo Payments, our payment provider. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show the money back on your statement.

Simulated purchases

Every other purchase button on the site is a simulation. No money is taken for them, so there is nothing to refund.
