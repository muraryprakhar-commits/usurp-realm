Free Browser Games to Play With Friends

The best game for a group is the one everybody can join in a minute. Every game here opens in a browser on a phone or a computer, with nothing to install and no account to make.

What makes a good game to play with friends

  • Nothing to install. If one friend has to download an app, make an account and wait for an update, you have lost them.
  • It works on phones. At a party, on a call or in a classroom, a phone is what everyone has.
  • One link gets everyone in. Sending a link in a group chat is easier than explaining a menu.

Here is what usurp.games offers for each way of playing together.

For a group, online: The Usurper

The Usurper is a medieval social deduction game for 4 to 10 players. Most players are loyal knights securing the keep; one or two are secretly the Usurper, sabotaging and striking when nobody is watching. When a knight falls, the council meets, talks and votes someone out.

  • Choose Private room and you get a four-letter code and an invite link. Friends open the link and they are in.
  • Computer knights fill any empty seats, so two or three friends still get a full match.
  • Private rooms allow free typing in the council. Public matches use preset quick-chat lines instead.
  • One player hosts the match in their browser and must keep the page open until it ends.

For a full walkthrough, see how to host a game night with The Usurper.

For two people, side by side: the board games

Two of the games have a two-player mode on one device: pass the phone or share the screen.

  • Viking chess (Hnefatafl): one player defends the king, the other leads the raiders, on the quick 7 by 7 board.
  • Nine Men's Morris: the old game of mills, quick to learn and hard to master.

Both also play against the computer at three levels, so one of you can practice first. New to them? Read the Hnefatafl rules or how to play Nine Men's Morris.

For friends anywhere: beat my score

The other games are single-player, but each one ends with a Challenge a friend button that sends your score with a link to the game.

  • The King's Archery Contest has a daily contest with the same winds for everyone that day, so scores compare fairly.
  • Siege: ten castles to topple with real physics, and stars for stones kept.
  • The Joust, Duel and Hold the Castle for quick rounds of timing, reflexes and defense.

All of them are free, with no blood, and they keep your best scores on your own device.
