## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Design your own coat of arms free in a minute, then pay once for print-ready files.
**What it does:** Crest Studio (usurp.games/crest.html) lets anyone build a heraldic coat of arms from 50 emblems,
real heraldic colors and divisions, shows its blazon and the meaning of every part, and sells the finished files.
**Product category:** Online coat of arms maker / family crest generator.
**Product type:** Browser-based design tool with paid digital downloads. No account needed.
**Business model:** Free to design, one-time purchase to download (via Dodo Payments):
- Custom Coat of Arms, $9: print-ready art, certificate of arms, profile picture, phone wallpaper
- Heirloom Crest Set, $19: engraving and line art, stamp and seal files, coloring page, bookplates, house banner, A4 certificate
- Crest Logo Kit, $29: transparent PNG and SVG, favicons, social banners, brand sheet
Usurp itself (the medieval rank-ladder game at /realm.html) is a free prototype. Buying artwork gives no rank or game advantage.

## Target Audience
**Primary use cases (each has its own landing page):**
- Families wanting a family crest (family-crest-maker, family-crest-generator, irish- and scottish-family-crest)
- Gift buyers: personalized crest with a "presented to" certificate (family-crest-gift)
- Couples: wedding crest (wedding-crest)
- Tabletop and fantasy players: D&D coat of arms, fantasy house names (dnd-coat-of-arms-generator, fantasy-house-name-generator)
- Clubs, teams, schools, small businesses and creators needing a crest logo (crest-logo-maker, clan-logo-maker, school-house-crests)
**Jobs to be done:** Get a meaningful, good-looking crest without hiring a designer; give a personal gift fast; give a group an identity.
**Geography:** Global, English-language. Priced in USD.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Custom heraldry from a designer is slow and costly; "your surname's crest" sites sell historical arms that usually do not belong to the buyer.
**Why alternatives fall short:** Not known yet (needs customer research).
**Emotional tension:** Wanting a sense of heritage, belonging or identity, and a gift that feels personal.

## Differentiation
- Free to design and preview, no signup; pay only for the files
- Shows the blazon and plain-English meaning of every color and emblem
- Original designs made by the buyer, honestly not presented as historical family arms
- One engine renders the Studio, free tools, share cards and e-cards, so share links preview the actual crest

## Objections
- "Is this my real family crest?" Answer honestly: it is an original design, not a historical grant of arms.

## Brand Voice
Plain, warm, a little medieval flavor without parody. Rules used across the site:
- US spelling; no em dashes; verified facts only; no invented history or statistics
- Clear prices stated up front; no dark patterns
