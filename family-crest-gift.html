Why a family crest makes a good gift

Most gifts are things. A crest is a little piece of identity: it takes what a family cares about and turns it into one emblem they can hang on the wall, print on mugs or pass down. Because you choose every part of it, it says "I thought about you" in a way a gift card never does.

  • Personal. Their family name, colors with meaning, an emblem that fits them and a motto they actually say.
  • Explained. The certificate of arms spells out the design in heraldic language (the blazon) and in plain English, so it starts conversations.
  • Instant. Files download the moment you pay, which makes it one of the few personalized gifts that works the night before.
  • Prints anywhere. Any local print shop, office store photo counter or online printer can print the files. No shipping from us.

Gift ideas by occasion

Christmas and the holidays

A framed crest for parents or in-laws, or the same crest on a set of mugs for every household in the family.

Weddings and anniversaries

Join two family names on one shield with the Per pale division, the old heraldic way of uniting two families. See the wedding crest guide.

A new home

A tower or key emblem for a housewarming print above the door, with the family name and the year they moved in as the motto.

Grandparents and family reunions

A crest for the whole family tree, printed on the reunion banner and T-shirts, with a framed copy for the grandparents who started it all.

Father's Day, Mother's Day and birthdays

A crest built around one person: their initial on the shield and a motto that is pure them.

How to give it

  1. Design it in Crest Studio. Need words? The Latin motto generator has 50+ mottos with translations, and the meanings guide explains every color and emblem.
  2. Add the gift line. Open "Giving it as a gift?" and type who it is for and who it is from. It prints on the certificate.
  3. Download the set for $9: print art, certificate, profile picture and phone wallpaper.
  4. Print and frame. The 4000 by 5000 px file prints at about 13 by 16 inches at full photo quality (300 pixels per inch), and comfortably at 16 by 20. The certificate is sized for US Letter.
Short on time? Email the files or the phone wallpaper with a note, then print a framed copy for later. It still counts.

Questions

Can I give it as a last-minute gift?

Yes. The files download as soon as payment goes through, so you can print or email them the same day.

Does the recipient need an account or anything else?

No. They receive ordinary image files (PNG and SVG) that open on any phone or computer.

Can I add a personal message?

Yes. Under "Giving it as a gift?" in the studio, add who it is presented to and who it is from. It prints on the certificate of arms along with the date.

Is it their real, historical family coat of arms?

No. It is original artwork you design for them in the style of heraldry, not an official grant of arms or a claim about their ancestors. Many people find that more meaningful, because it describes the family as it is today.
