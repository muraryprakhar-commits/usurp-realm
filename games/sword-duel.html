Sword Fighting Game: Duel

Read the blade. Parry. Riposte. How fast are your hands?

How to play

  1. Your opponent raises their sword to your left, overhead or to your right, then strikes.
  2. Parry the same way before the blade lands: tap the left, high or right button, swipe, or use the arrow keys.
  3. Watch for feints: some knights start one way and strike another. Guess early and you will be fooled.
  4. Parry just before the blow lands for a perfect parry, then tap the glowing mark to riposte. Land enough ripostes and the knight yields.
Phone and tabletTap the three buttons, or swipe left, up or right. Tap the glowing mark to riposte.
ComputerArrow keys or A, W and D to parry. Space to riposte.

Tips

  • Do not guess. Wait until the blade has clearly moved, then parry.
  • Perfect parries come from patience: block in the last instant before the strike.
  • Every fourth parry in a row also opens a riposte.
  • Beating a knight wins back a lost heart.

Questions

Is this a reaction time test?

Partly. It times how long you take to parry once the blade shows where it is going, so it rewards quick reading as well as quick hands. Feints keep it fair: guessing does not work.

Is it violent?

No blood. Knights clash, parry and yield.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, with big buttons and swipes, in any modern browser.
