        The Law of the Realm

        The Charter

        Why this stays a status product and not a wager.

        ⚜
        I

        No chance in rank

        Rank is set only by lifetime spend.

        II

        No cash payout

        Spend never returns as money, and no Court Game rewards cash.

        III

        No cash value

        Titles and any in-game currency are non-transferable, and non-refundable except where law requires.

        IV

        No fake users

        The Founding Houses are labelled lore, not customers.

        The spec flags a real Terms of Service and legal review before meaningful money moves, including the US market and India's Online Gaming Act (2025). This page is not legal advice.
