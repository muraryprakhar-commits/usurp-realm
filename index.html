        The Charter

        Why this stays a status product and not a wager.

        No chance element in rank. Rank is set only by lifetime spend.
        No cash payout, ever. Spend never returns as money, and no Court Game rewards cash.
        Titles and any in-game currency have no cash value, are non-transferable, and are non-refundable except where law requires.
        No fake users. The Founding Houses are labelled lore, not customers.

        The spec flags a real Terms of Service and legal review before meaningful money moves, including the US market and India's Online Gaming Act (2025). This page is not legal advice.

        Roadmap

        1. Phase 0, lock the spec. Finalize structure, pick the name, draft the Terms line on titles having no cash value.
        2. Phase 1, MVP. Leaderboard, Stripe checkout for rank purchases, basic profiles.
        3. Phase 2, Founding Era. Real beta cohort and 1 to 2 lore Houses, Peasant signup open, first real paying customers.
        4. Phase 3, brand layer. Open House sponsorships to real small businesses.
        5. Phase 4, engagement. Court Games, seasonal resets, referrals.
