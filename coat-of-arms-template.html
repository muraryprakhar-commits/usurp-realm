Family crest activity (30 to 45 minutes)

  1. Warm up (5 min). Show a few coats of arms and ask what each one tells you about its owner. The heraldry meanings guide has examples.
  2. Fill the quarters (15 min). On the worksheet, draw: something your family loves to do; where your family comes from; a value your family lives by; an object that represents you.
  3. Write a motto (5 min). A short phrase your family says, or a Latin motto from the motto generator.
  4. Color with meaning (10 min). Pick two colors from the meanings guide and explain why.
  5. Share (5 to 10 min). Each student presents their crest in three sentences.

The PDFs are US Letter with margins that also print fine on A4. The PNG and SVG versions can be resized for anything.
