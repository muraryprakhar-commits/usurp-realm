### Day 6 · Mon 2 Nov · The surname coat of arms myth
- **LinkedIn + X thread:** honest myth-busting. In most heraldic traditions arms belonged to a person, not to everyone with a surname, so "your family's crest" prints are usually someone else's arms. Make your own instead.
- Link: `/family-crest-maker.html` · Research: verify the claim with authoritative heraldry sources (e.g. College of Arms, Court of the Lord Lyon); note traditions that differ, such as Polish clan arms.
