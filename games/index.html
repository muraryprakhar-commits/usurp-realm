Questions

Are the games free?

Yes. All four games are free to play in your browser, with no download, no account and no purchase needed.

Do they work on phones?

Yes. Every game is designed for touch first and also plays with a mouse and keyboard. Use the full-screen button under a game for the best view on a phone.

Are the games suitable for children?

Yes. There is no blood or gore: arrows hit targets, lances shatter, knights yield and walls crumble.

Do you save anything about me?

Only your best scores and sound setting, kept in your own browser. There are no accounts. See the privacy policy.

What does my coat of arms have to do with it?

Design a coat of arms in Crest Studio and every game uses it: on your shield in the duel and the joust, on the banners of your castle and beside your score in the archery contest.
